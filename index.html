Service Offerings

Energy Operators

AEMBank provides structured financing and advisory support to energy operators and service providers across the hydrocarbon and gas value chain, including upstream exploration, gas development projects, midstream infrastructure and downstream processing. The Bank supports commercially viable projects that strengthen energy security, industrialisation and regional value addition within member states.

Service Offerings

Maritime Projects

AEMBank delivers structured financing and advisory support for maritime and offshore assets that enable resource development, trade and energy logistics. This includes vessel operators, Floating Production Storage and Offloading units (FPSOs), charter providers, offshore support services and port linked infrastructure critical to efficient regional supply chains.
