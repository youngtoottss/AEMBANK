AEMBank Joins the Association of African Development Finance Institutions

AEMBank formalises its membership in the continental development finance community

August 2026 AADFI Membership

Following an invitation by the Association of African Development Finance Institutions (AADFI), the African Extractive Minerals Development Bank (AEMBank) is pleased to announce its acceptance and admission into AADFI.

AADFI is a continental body established to promote cooperation among African development finance institutions and strengthen their contribution to sustainable economic development across the continent. Membership provides opportunities for institutional collaboration, knowledge exchange, capacity building and engagement with leading continental development finance institutions.

This important milestone reflects AEMBank's commitment to building strong strategic partnerships and advancing the sustainable development of Africa's extractive minerals sector.
